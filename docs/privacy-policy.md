# Clean Sweep Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Clean Sweep does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

- **Photos:** the before and after photos you take or choose are stored only on your device, inside the app. They are never uploaded.
- **Progress and ratings:** room ratings and cleaning progress are stored only on your device.
- **Notifications:** if you allow them, the app schedules one local notification to tell you when the timer ends. It is created on your device and involves no server.
- **No accounts, analytics, advertising or third-party tracking.**

You can delete everything at any time with "Reset Everything" in the app, or by deleting the app.
